# My Week 1 agent portfolio

I built Luna, a small public-information research assistant. Its task is to produce a short, sourced brief and save it for human review.

This portfolio explains the task, procedure, observed result and one correction. It contains curated public material, not the private runtime configuration.

## What remains my decision

I choose the task and check the sources. Publishing, sending messages and connecting other accounts require a separate decision. A saved draft is not a published result.
